Know Your Options

Mainland vs. Freezone vs. Offshore

Compare the key features of UAE jurisdictions to choose the optimal foundation for your enterprise.

Feature UAE Freezone Company (Recommended) UAE Mainland LLC Offshore / Holding Co.
Foreign Ownership 100% Foreign Ownership 100% Foreign Ownership (Commercial) 100% Foreign Ownership
Local Market Trading Within Freezone & Internationally (or via local distributor) Anywhere across UAE Mainland & Government tenders International only (No UAE domestic trade)
Corporate Tax (Qualifying) 0% on Qualifying Freezone Income 9% on Net Profit > AED 375,000 0% (Non-resident tax treatment)
Residency & Investor Visas 1 to Unlimited Visas (Based on office allocation) Unlimited (Based on office sq. footage) No Visas Issued
Office Space Requirement Flexi-Desk / Virtual Desk Allowed Physical Office / Ejari Required Registered Agent Address Only
Setup Timeframe 3 – 5 Business Days 5 – 7 Business Days 7 – 10 Business Days

UAE Formation Document Checklist

For Individual Shareholders:

  • Valid Passport Copy (minimum 6 months validity)
  • UAE Entry Stamp or Visit Visa copy (if in UAE)
  • Passport Size Photo with white background
  • 3 Suggested Company Trade Names
  • Brief Description of Business Activity

For Corporate / Holding Shareholders:

  • Certificate of Incorporation (Attested)
  • Memorandum & Articles of Association (MOA/AOA)
  • Board Resolution approving the UAE subsidiary
  • Power of Attorney for authorized signatory
